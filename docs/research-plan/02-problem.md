**Pick A.** B becomes a feature (gap map). C and D are mentioned as future value only.

## 5 Whys on A
1. Why are safety decisions slow? → Answers need digging through many tests and papers.
2. Why? → Results are stored per experiment, in different formats, with no common schema.
3. Why does that matter more now? → Moon/Mars habitats have conditions few tests covered (partial gravity, ~34% O₂ at 8.2 psia).
4. Why can't Earth tests cover it? → Earth certification runs in 1 g; microgravity can lower the oxygen level at which materials burn.
5. Why is that dangerous? → A material can pass on Earth and still burn in space. **Root problem confirmed, not a symptom.**
