# 06 · User validation — fast version (at the venue, Fri)

No time for a survey. Do **3 chats of 5 minutes** with mentors or judges-in-the-room who have engineering or science backgrounds. Write quotes down word for word.

## Mom Test questions (about their past, not our idea)
1. "When did you last need a safety or test result from a research paper? What did you do?"
2. "How long did it take to find the number you needed?"
3. "When you read a result, how do you decide if it applies to your situation?"
4. (Only at the end) "If a tool gave you an answer plus how close the evidence is to your case — what would make you trust it?"

## Who
Space Apps Cairo mentors (Discord #mentorship), any SUT faculty in mechanical/aerospace, any mentor with materials or safety work.
