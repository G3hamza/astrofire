# 01 · Brief — understand the game

## What the Cairo judges score (official guidelines)
Five criteria. No weights are published → **[assumption] equal weight (20% each).**

| Criterion | What judges look for | How StarryNight scores |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | Real problem, clear beneficiaries, positive impact | Crew fire safety for Moon/Mars habitats; engineers, crews, NASA researchers |
| **Creativity** | Original idea or fresh angle, inventive use of tech | Evidence-distance confidence score, hidden-risk finder, skeptic agent, next-experiment recommender |
| **Validity** | Sound science, realistic, demonstrated, claims supported | Working prototype, cited NASA sources, honest "low confidence" outside tested data |
| **Relevance** | Solves the actual challenge, meaningful NASA data use | Directly does what the brief asks: summarize, rank, explain PSI combustion results with AI |
| **Presentation** | Clear problem → solution → value, good visuals | 16-slide deck (cut to ~10), dashboard demo, 30 s video |

## Constraints and rules
- **Submission fields (Cairo):** Team name · Challenge · Demo link · Proposal link · Project link (deployed) · Official team page (global Space Apps site) · Subjects (comma-separated).
- **Global Space Apps rules:** demo video **≤ 30 s** (or ≤ 7 slides), **English subtitles**, public link. AI-generated images/video must be **labelled**, and AI tools used must be **disclosed**. [Space Apps submission guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/)
- **Pre-submit checklist (Cairo):** challenge addressed · NASA open data used meaningfully · feasible solution · description + materials ready · **all links accessible** · AI-use disclosure followed.

## Past winners — what they had in common
- 2025: 10 global winners from 11,500+ submissions; one from Egypt (Zumorroda-X, Art & Technology). [NASA, 2025 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/)
- Best Use of Science 2025 (SpaceGenes+) was an **interactive dashboard** on astronaut health — same format as ours.
- Pattern **[assumption from reading the winners list]:** one clear user, real NASA data visibly used, a working visual demo, one memorable idea.

## Win thesis
> **We win if we show a working tool that turns real NASA fire experiments into a cited, confidence-rated answer for a Moon or Mars habitat — and catches a material that passes Earth tests but can burn in space — to judges who care about scientific validity and meaningful NASA data use.**

## Global award targets (after Cairo nomination)
Best Use of Data (primary) · Best Use of Science (secondary). [Space Apps awards](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/)
