## Root causes (fishbone, short)
- **Process:** certification in 1 g; space tests rare and expensive.
- **Product (data):** per-experiment formats, no shared schema, papers vs tables.
- **Place:** Moon 0.17 g, Mars 0.38 g — almost untested.
- **People:** few combustion experts; engineers lack time to read decades of papers.
- **Policy/Culture:** atmosphere choices (8.2 psia / 34%) trade spacewalk prep against fire risk.

## Why now
Artemis-era habitats + proposed high-O₂ atmospheres + LLMs that can read papers and cite them.
