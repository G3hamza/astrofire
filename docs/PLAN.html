What the NASA data already says

The homes we're planning have never been fire-tested in space.

Every solid-fuel space test in our data ran at 13.6–22.2% oxygen near ISS cabin pressure. NASA's exploration atmosphere (8.2 psia, 34% O₂) and the Moon and Mars habitat design points sit in cells with zero space tests.

Space isn't simply "safer" or "more dangerous". It depends on airflow and material.

In SAFFIRE-II at 20 cm/s, two silicone samples that burned completely in Earth gravity barely burned in space, while the cotton-fiberglass fabric burned fully in both. That is why engineers need evidence for their exact conditions, not a rule of thumb.

Airflow decides whether a flame lives.

BASS-II notes record 19 flames that went out as airflow was reduced or the fan was turned off, and 7 that blew off when airflow was raised. That's the basis for the fan advisor.
